About Me

Hi, I’m Lana Gvirjishvili, an exchange student from Georgia currently spending an exchange year in Miller, South Dakota. This journey has helped me grow personally and academically, giving me the chance to step out of my comfort zone, make lifelong connections, and better understand the world around me.

I’m passionate about communication, leadership, and learning about different cultures. I speak five languages—Georgian, Russian, English, Turkish, and Armenian—and I understand even more! Language has always been my gateway to connecting with people and building bridges between cultures.

When I return home, I’ll be graduating and starting university to study International Communications. I’m planning to specialize in intercultural relations and media, and I’m seriously considering coming back to the U.S. for my senior year to earn a second diploma and broaden my international experience even more.

In the future, I hope to work in diplomacy, international media, or global non-profits. I want to learn more languages and travel the world to explore new cultures, meet inspiring people, and make a meaningful difference wherever I go. Outside of academics, I enjoy photography, reading, traveling, gardening, and making wine with my grandpa, which are special moments I really enjoy.

This exchange year has been a powerful reminder that the world is full of opportunity, and I can’t wait to see where life takes me next.

Projects During My Exchange Year

Volunteering Certificate

I earned this certificate after completing over 100 hours of volunteer work during my exchange year. This experience taught me the value of community service and helped me connect with people from diverse backgrounds.

Member of National Honor Society

I became a member of the National Honor Society during my exchange year. It means I did well in school, helped others, and showed good character. I'm really proud of it because it shows my hard work and how much I care about learning and helping my community.

Job Shadowing at Lunchbox

In my Employability class, our final project was to do job shadowing anywhere we wanted. I chose Lunchbox because I was really interested in how things work in a kitchen. I got to help make pizzas and cookies, and I learned about all the different jobs there. It was a great experience, and now I want to start my own pizza business someday.
